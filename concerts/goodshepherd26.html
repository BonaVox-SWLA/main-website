Good Shepherd Summer Concert Series -- Journey: Beside Still Waters

Tuesday June 9 7:30 - 9:00 pm Lake Charles, LA

Good Shepherd Episcopal Lake Charles LA, 715 Kirkman St, Lake Charles, LA 70601, USA

BonaVox returns to Good Shepherd’s Summer Music Festival for 2026 with beloved favorites and a first look at exciting new music for the coming season. Our concert will be a celebration of five centuries of choral music — joyful, searching, hopeful, alive. The program will include selections ranging from the Renaissance to the Classical period, and American favorites from the Sacred Harp.

Tickets are $20 at the door, and season memberships are available for $60. Children under the age of 12 will be admitted free of charge. Season tickets can also be purchased online at www.GoodShepherd-LC.com (click on “Events” and then “2026 Summer Music Festival”). Checks can be mailed to the church, made out to Good Shepherd, with “Summer Music Festival” in the memo line.
