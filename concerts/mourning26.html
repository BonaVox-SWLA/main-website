Dreams in Bloom

Friday November 13 7:00 - 8:30 pm Lake Charles, LA

St. Andrew Presbyterian Church Lake Charles LA, 4020 Hodges Street, Lake Charles, LA 70605, USA

An intimate recital from members of the BonaVox collective-- exploring themes of dreaming, hope, honoring the past and looking toward the future.

Featuring music from Wicked, Fauré, Les Misérables, Bette Midler, Strauss, and more. Come enjoy a night of singing and piano music! Performances by Kate Colby, MaryKate Core, and Geoff Stemen.
