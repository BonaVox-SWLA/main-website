Our Goals

we have so many goals and opportunities!

1

As part of our mission to provide access to high-quality music performances, BonaVox intends to bring a free concert to a retirement community, hospital or prison each concert series.

We do not ask these audiences for financial support, and would appreciate if the wider community could help offset these costs.

2

BonaVox would love to produce an award-winning album. Lets get a Grammy!

3

BonaVox wants to ensure a pipeline for college-level music students to achieve their professional goals

Help us fund a scholarship that includes mentorship from our members and professional development opportunities to support their future careers.
