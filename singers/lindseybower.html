Lindsey Bower

@lalalinset
Soprano Social Media & Marketing Coordinator

Lindsey Bower started singing from a very young age and developed a love for music, performing, and songwriting. She graduated from McNeese State University with a Bachelor's in Vocal Performance in 2019, working as the choir librarian and singing in the McNeese Chamber Singers, Women’s ensemble, and Opera Workshop. She has sung at First United Methodist Church with the Chancel Choir, played handbells with the Asbury Ringers, and worked with the Music Ministries program. As a soloist and chorus member, she's performed with the McNeese Chamber Singers, the Louisiana Choral Foundation, and Louisiana Vocal Arts Ensemble (LVAE). She completed her Masters in Music at Edinburgh Napier University in Scotland in 2025 and premiered 3 part a cappella arrangements of music by Connie Converse as a part of BonaVox's new concert series, Conversations.
