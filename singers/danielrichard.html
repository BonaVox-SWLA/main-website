Daniel Richard

Tenor

Daniel Richard is a native of Lafayette, Louisiana and was raised in a household of musicians. He is an alumni of the University of Louisiana at Lafayette with a B.S in Business. During his time as UL, he traveled around south Louisiana and Mississippi performing with the UL Lafayette Chamber Singers. He also spent 3 years performing early music and visiting historic venues around the state with the Baton Rouge Early Vocal Ensemble. He currently sings as cantor and a tenor with the Our Lady Queen of Angels Catholic Church choir in Opelousas. In his free time, Daniel enjoys photography and thrifting.
