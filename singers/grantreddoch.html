Grant Reddoch

@greddoch95
Tenor Press Relations

Grant Reddoch was born and raised in Lake Charles, LA, in a family and community that loves music, from 80's rock to church hymns on Sunday. During high school he continued studying piano and taught himself the guitar to serve as a musician at retreats throughout the Episcopal Diocese of Western Louisiana. After graduating from St. Louis Catholic High School, he went on to pursue a Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering at Louisiana Tech University. While there he joined the Catholic Church in 2014 and grew as a vocalist by singing with the Catholic student center choir throughout the week and learning master works with the Louisiana Tech Chorus (Dubois' The Seven Last Words of Christ, Durufle's Requiem, Mozart's Coronation Mass, Haydn's Lord Nelson Mass, Rutter's Gloria).

Currently he covers parts ranging from upper tenor to low bass depending on the needs of the group he is singing with, including the Immaculate Conception Cathedral Chorale and BonaVox. He also plays guitar, piano, and sings contemporary music for the local Catholic young adult community nights (Fully Alive!) and serves as a cantor at Immaculate Conception Cathedral. In his spare time, he likes to run, cook, write, and play music. Grant is currently working on producing his own music including singer/songwriter and choral works for release in the next year. His setting of In Paradisum was premiered by BonaVox in the 2024 Mourning series at St. Philip Neri Catholic Church in Kinder, LA.

Find Grant on Instagram, LinkedIn, or send any arrangement or score inquiries to grant.reddoch@bonavoxswla.org.
